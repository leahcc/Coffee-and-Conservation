2025 Photos and People

I conduct my dissertation research in close collaboration with the Smithsonian Tropical Research Institute and with the Smithsonian Migratory Bird Center. Specifically, Dr. Ruth Bennett and the Smithsonian Bird Friendly Project have supported me enormously.

2025 Field Season | Gualaca, Chiriquí, Panamá

In 2025, I worked at the Smithsonian’s Fortuna Field Station. My team included Stefany Flores and Josué Justo with volunteer appearances from {Nathanya Goudreau}(https://qcbs.ca/fr/membre-etudiant/?student=3598) and Dr. Maureen McClung. Finally, we worked alongside the Fortuna Field Station’s permanent staff including station manager Carlos Espinosa and botanist Bady Garcia (whose tree diversity work you can see featured on France24 News here!).
